## Personalisation notes

- Cite the specific AI tool the firm uses if publicly known (Harvey, CoCounsel, Lexis+ AI).

- Bar associations in most jurisdictions have now issued guidance on AI supervision. Reference the relevant one (ABA Formal Opinion 512 for US, SRA guidance for UK, Law Council for AU).
