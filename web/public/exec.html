Executive briefing · confidential

The system of record for AI agents.

Enterprises are handing real decisions to AI agents — refunds, approvals, escalations, trades. None can yet reproduce, prove, or govern what those agents did. Runback is the platform that makes every agent decision re-runnable, tamper-evident, and gated — in the customer's own perimeter.

The problem

Agents are making decisions no one can explain — or undo.

An agent chains dozens of model calls and tool actions per run. When one goes wrong, logs show what happened, never why. You can't reproduce the incident, prove the fix, or show a control was in place. For a regulated enterprise, that's unbounded operational, legal, and reputational exposure on every automated decision.

Why now

Two curves are colliding — adoption and regulation.

Adoption

Agents are going to production

The shift from chatbots to autonomous agents that take actions is the fastest-moving enterprise software wave since cloud. Every one of them needs a record.

Regulation

The mandate is dated

EU AI Act Art. 12 logging is enforceable Aug 2026. APRA CPS 230, UNECE DSSAD, NIST AI RMF all now require traceability of automated decisions.

The forcing function isn't optional, and it's on the calendar. The buyer has to solve this — the only question is with what.

The opportunity

Governance is to agents what observability was to cloud.

Every cloud workload needed observability — and that mandatory layer built Datadog (~$40B), Splunk, New Relic. Every autonomous agent needs governance: a system of record. Same inevitability, a category forming right now, with the regulatory deadline pulling demand forward.

The category

Observability shows you logs. We let you re-run them.

A flight recorder versus a flight simulator. Existing tools record what an agent did and stop there. Runback re-executes any decision deterministically, gates the change against policy, and seals a signed record nothing can alter. That's the difference between watching your agents and being accountable for them.

Security, trust & procurement · for your CISO

Built so your auditors — not just your engineers — sign off.

Data never leaves

Self-host in your VPC with one docker compose up. Secrets & PII redacted in-process before egress. Your store, your retention, your keys.

Access & identity

OIDC SSO — Okta, Entra, Google, Auth0, Ping — and owner/admin/member/viewer RBAC. API keys & session tokens stored only as hashes.
